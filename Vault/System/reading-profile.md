# Reading profile

## Primary interests

- Generative modelling for physical systems
- Bayesian inverse problems
- Data assimilation
- Scientific machine learning
- PDE surrogate modelling
- Physics-constrained learning
- Structure-preserving learning
- Scientific and physical foundation models
- Sim-to-real transfer for physical systems

## Especially valuable papers

A paper is especially valuable when it:

- Introduces reusable methodology rather than only an isolated application.
- Addresses sparse, noisy, or indirect observations.
- Contains careful comparisons, ablations, or uncertainty analysis.
- Studies physical consistency or preservation of invariants.
- Discusses generalization, adaptation, transfer, or sim-to-real behaviour.
- Provides open-source code or sufficiently detailed algorithms.
- Connects generative modelling with inverse problems or data assimilation.

## Lower-priority papers

A paper is generally lower priority when it:

- Reports only a small benchmark improvement.
- Applies a standard model without methodological insight.
- Focuses on a domain with little transfer to physical systems.
- Depends primarily on scale without analysing why the method works.
- Provides conclusions unsupported by suitable baselines or ablations.

## Recommendation policy

Use `must-read` sparingly.


Prefer `uncertain` over an overconfident recommendation when:

- Only an abstract was available.
- PDF extraction was poor.
- Essential evidence was contained mainly in figures.
- The paper’s relevance depends on details not available in the extracted text.
