# Research synthesis

Define the question and corpus, then use `research_search` and `research_context` for relevant papers and evidence. State corpus searched, screening choices, each paper's evidence mode, and limitations. Include user-named papers and disclose unavailable text.

Compare assumptions, methods, populations/data, outcomes, findings, and limitations on commensurate terms. Trace material claims to original sections/pages where available. Distinguish author claims, cross-paper interpretation, and unresolved disagreement. Metadata/abstract evidence may guide screening but cannot support body-level claims.

Read [references/output.md](references/output.md). Save the separate AI-owned synthesis with `research_save_artifact(kind="synthesis")`. Never rewrite reviews, human notes, or briefs, and create curation proposals only when asked.
